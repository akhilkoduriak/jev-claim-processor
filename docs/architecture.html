Smart Insurance Claim Processor: Architecture

How a claim moves from the browser to a decision and into storage
Browser
React app · frontend/ · localhost:3000

App.js

Side bar navigation and shared state. Polls decisions and engine status every 2 s.

ClaimForm

Single claim: one claim at a time.
Test datasets: Quick (3), Comprehensive (32), Fraud checks (11), with expected outcomes.

Overview

Outcomes, claim value, engine health, policy-rule reviews and test matches.

Claims

Search and filter every decision. Expand a row for the assessment, engine and reasons.

API server
Node.js + Express · backend/server.js · localhost:5000

REST routes

POST/api/claims/process
GET/api/claims · /api/decisions · /api/decisions/:id
GET/api/stats · /api/engine · /api/datasets · /health
POST/api/reset

Decision engine backend/decision-engine.js

Tries Jev first. Uses the rules if Jev is off, has no key, or the call fails. Then applies the same policy rules to either result.

Jev client jev-client.js
Sends the claim and 6 questions to /systemone. Maps the answers to a decision, confidence and alert level.
Fallback rules rules.js
Care type, medical necessity, price check and billing check.

Policy rules: approvals of $50,000+ and decisions under 60% confidence go to review.

Decision record

decision approve / deny / review · confidence 0–1 · alertLevel · reasons · recommendedAction · processingTimeMs · usedRealAPI

External service
Called over HTTPS

Jev API (TypeSafe AI)

POST /v1/systemone. Answers every question in one pass. Authenticated with a Bearer key.

If the call fails

The claim goes to the fallback rules, so a decision is always returned. After a rejected key, Jev is skipped for 60 s.

Local files
JSON on disk

data/

claims.json: claims as submitted
decisions.json: one decision per claim, with engine and reasons

sample-claims.json

3 test datasets (46 claims) with expected outcomes. Used by the app and load-samples.js.
